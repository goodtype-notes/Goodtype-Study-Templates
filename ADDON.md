# Study Templates

Set up a calculation, lab report or study page with paper made for the job.

## Paper layouts

- **Engineering calculation paper** pairs a 5 mm grid with heavier lines every centimetre and a margin for notes.
- **Lab report** leaves a header for the experiment and uses ruled lines below.
- **Cornell grid notes** gives you a cue column, a squared main area and a ruled summary band.
- **Dotted bullet journal** uses a 5 mm dot grid.
- **Exam and solution sheet** leaves a wide left margin beside the ruled answer area.
- **Graph paper** uses a 2 mm grid with heavier lines every centimetre.

## Pen palettes

**Engineering** covers seven everyday drawing colours. **Soft highlighters** offers six pale tones. **Colour-blind safe** includes the eight Okabe–Ito colours.

Install the pack from **Addons**. Find the paper layouts when adding a page and the palettes in the pen colour picker.
